## What is LLTHW?

Learn Languages the Hard Way is a public experiment in self-directed language acquisition.

Rather than chasing shortcuts, we focus on:

- Daily practice
- Structural understanding
- Deliberate vocabulary acquisition
- Long-term consistency
- Public accountability
